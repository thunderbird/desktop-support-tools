Check Thunderbird account settings

Paste Thunderbird's Troubleshooting Information and get one verdict per account: which settings are wrong, what to change them to, and where in Thunderbird to change them.

Nothing you paste leaves this page. There is no server and no storage: the checking happens in your browser, and account names and email addresses are discarded before anything is displayed.

How to get the Troubleshooting Information

Thunderbird app menu ☰ → Help → Troubleshooting Information, then Copy text to clipboard. The app menu is the ☰ button in the top right — not every platform shows a menu bar. Paste the whole thing below, or just the lines under Mail and News Accounts, which is all this tool reads.

Leave Include account names unticked. It is off by default, and everything needed to check a configuration is in the copy either way.

Prefer the command line? check_settings.py runs exactly these checks in a terminal, reading the same expected settings — useful for scripting, or for checking a dump you already have in a file. How to use it, and how to set it up.

Expected settings and their provenance live in settings.json, shared with the command-line version. Source: thunderbird/desktop-support-tools. Licensed MPL-2.0.
